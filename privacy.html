Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-10. MeFauna is a friends-first app for people you have really met. This page says what we store, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

MeFauna is run by Jonathan Sorelius, a private person in Helsingborg, Sweden (the "data controller"). Contact: jonathan.sorelius@gmail.com. We answer in English or Swedish.

What we store and why

Account: your email and password (stored as a secure hash). Needed to run your account (contract).

Profile: name, home city, photo, birth date. Your birth date is private; we use it for the age rules and, if you want, birthday reminders to your contacts (day and month only).

Contacts and meetings: who you added, at which level, and when and roughly where you met (city, rounded place).

Chats, calls, photos, videos and voice notes: end-to-end encrypted. Our server only stores them scrambled, plus who is in a chat and when something was sent. We cannot read them.

Moments, posts, replies, likes, events and communities you join or create, shown to the people you choose.

Location sharing, only if you turn it on (consent): your position rounded to about 4 km, shown to your Friends and Close friends. Turn it off any time in Settings.

Phones: a name such as "Android phone", when it was last used, and the public encryption keys that let others send you encrypted chats.

Reports, removals and appeals: to keep MeFauna safe and because the EU Digital Services Act requires it.

What we never do

No ads, no selling or renting your data, no trackers or analytics, no profiling for marketing.

Who can see it and who helps us

Other users see what your settings allow. We do not share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it (for example a court order).

Supabase stores our database, files and logins on servers in Stockholm (EU), under a data processing agreement.

For calls, phones connect directly; a STUN server (Google) only sees your IP address to set up the connection.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or your account. When you delete your account, everything is removed at once; copies in our backups disappear within 30 days.

Messages you sent stay with the people you sent them to, on their phones.

Closed reports are kept for up to 12 months so repeated abuse can be handled.

Your rights

Get a copy of your data (Settings → Account → Download my data), correct it (edit your profile), delete it (Settings → Account → Delete my account), object to or limit how we use it, move it elsewhere, and withdraw consent (for example location sharing) at any time.

For anything else, write to jonathan.sorelius@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

You can complain to a data protection authority: IMY in Sweden (imy.se), Datatilsynet in Norway or Denmark, or the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland (tietosuoja.fi).

Children and teens

You must be 16 or older. People under 18 get extra protection (for example, they can’t start communities or events).

Security

End-to-end encryption (MLS) for chats and media, encrypted storage, strict access rules for every table, and logins with secure passwords. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authority within 72 hours where required.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will show you the new version first.
